# Third-party notices / 第三方项目致谢

This project was designed after review of [RedactShot](https://github.com/darkzOGx/RedactShot), a MIT-licensed browser extension for on-device screenshot redaction.

Its redaction policy (solid fill, pixel padding and bounds clamping), sensitive-data detection categories, and human-review-first principles informed our implementation. The current application is a **new, separate Chinese browser editor**, not an official RedactShot release or a direct fork of its Chrome extension.
